Questions

Good to know

Do I need gym equipment to start? +

No. The training course starts with bodyweight calisthenics you can do at home and builds up gradually. When you're ready for a barbell, the planner walks you through it.

How does the photo calorie scanner work? +

You snap your plate and the app estimates the calories and protein. Photo estimates aren't perfect, so you confirm the portion in one tap — and the food recognition is tuned for the dishes people actually eat, including West-African meals.

Is a calorie deficit safe? +

Overload caps the deficit at a sensible level, holds a minimum daily calorie floor, and is built for adults 18 and over. It's designed to lose fat while protecting muscle and energy — not crash dieting.

Which phones does it support? +

It's built for iPhone and Android, syncing steps from Apple Health and Google Health Connect, plus most smartwatches and fitness bands.

Can I run it for my whole gym? +

Yes — the Gym plan gives your members gym-vs-gym challenges and a branded leaderboard, with an owner dashboard to keep everyone engaged and returning.
